Downforce: Wet Race

Privacy Policy · Effective 14 November 2026

Downforce is a single-player racing game. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

Your settings (lap count, difficulty, camera, steering and gear options) are saved locally on your device so they are remembered next time. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Device sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your device's motion sensor while you drive, only to steer the car. Sensor readings are used instantly and never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play store page.
